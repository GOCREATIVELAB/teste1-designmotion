# GO Creative Lab — motion style guide

## Visual grammar

- Dark green-black field with olive/lime brand accent.
- Ivory typography with compact monospace metadata.
- Editorial asymmetry: large type anchors the upper field while structured systems occupy the lower field.
- Thin rules, circular marks and cards express movement without decorative particle noise.
- Motion is deliberate: short editorial reveals, restrained spring settling and camera-like scale changes.

## Type and layout

- Primary: system Arial/Helvetica stack for deterministic local rendering.
- Secondary: Courier New for metadata and process labels.
- Keep the primary headline inside the central 84% safe width.
- Keep the top brand lockup and bottom metadata clear of the 8% outer margin.

## Brand usage

Use the downloaded official logo and symbol from `assets/`. Do not recreate the mark with text or alter its proportions.
